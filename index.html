What we do

Practical support for everyday stability.

We focus on two essentials that help residents move forward: reliable access to food and the confidence to manage money.

01

Food security

Working with food banks and collecting food donations to stock sober-living houses and homeless shelters across Long Island.

02

Financial literacy

Grant-funded programs teaching budgeting, saving, and money skills for long-term stability and independence for residents of group homes and shelters.

Supporting the places that help people rebuild.

We serve sober-living homes and homeless shelters on Long Island, New York, strengthening the day-to-day support available to residents.
